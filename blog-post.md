# How I Set Up My Cybersecurity Portfolio

Building my cybersecurity portfolio taught me that technical skills alone are not enough in today’s job market. Employers and recruiters also want to see professionalism, initiative, and proof of hands-on work. I started by purchasing my own domain and configuring it through Cloudflare so I could create a professional email address tied to my cybersecurity identity. This also gave me practical experience working with DNS records, nameservers, and email routing.

After setting up my domain, I created a GitHub Pages portfolio site to publicly showcase my interests, technical background, and future cybersecurity projects. Even though the website is simple right now, it represents the beginning of my professional online presence. I also learned that documenting projects and building a public portfolio can help demonstrate initiative and technical growth to employers.

This project helped me better understand how branding and technical skills connect in cybersecurity. Moving forward, I plan to continue adding labs, projects, and technical write-ups related to digital forensics and cyber investigations.
